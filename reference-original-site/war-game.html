War game

Iran theater, 2026. You are the senior U.S. decision-maker. Each week you issue one military order, choose one diplomatic track, and direct one covert operation. Six actors — Iran, Houthis, Hezbollah, Israel, Saudi Arabia — respond. Your goal: reopen the chokepoints, contain the conflict, avoid a global recession.

01

Issue military orders

Deploy forces, authorize strikes, or hold defensive posture. Each option costs resources and shifts the balance.

02

Choose a diplomatic track

Activate back-channels, negotiate ceasefires, or coordinate with allies. Diplomacy builds capital that kinetic action spends.

03

Direct covert operations

Cyber attacks, HUMINT expansion, economic warfare. High impact, low visibility — but slow to take effect.

04

Watch the world react

Iran, the Houthis, Hezbollah, Saudi Arabia, and Israel respond. Oil prices shift. Chokepoints open or close. Your choices cascade.
