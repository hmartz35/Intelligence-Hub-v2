04About & methodologyAcademic simulation exercise

Methodology

This simulation centers on a fictional 2025–2026 conflict scenario involving Houthi Red Sea attacks, U.S.–Iran hostilities, and principals-level decision-making. Tools and briefings are written using authentic formatting and analytical frameworks drawn from publicly available references including CJCSM 3160.01A, JP 3-60, NARA RG 243, NSArchive at GWU, and the Senate Intelligence Committee drone report.

All tools use transparent quantitative models — Monte Carlo simulation with Box–Muller normal draws; Bayesian updating via likelihood ratios; Keplerian orbital propagation; logistic-regression CPTs with forward sampling; pure-strategy Nash equilibrium solving; utility-weighted agent-based decision models. All calculations run client-side.

Fictional elements are explicitly marked throughout. Retroactive simulation exercise for academic and educational purposes. No classified or restricted information is used or represented.

    First-time reader · How to use this portfolio

    Welcome to the National Security Simulation

    This is an academic simulation of NSC principals-level decision-making during the 2025–2026 Houthi Red Sea crisis and 2026 Iran War. The portfolio combines 11 interactive tools and 4 PDF briefing books, each designed to be explored independently or as a coherent set.

    A common path: start with the Day-15 Briefing to understand the scenario, then use the Orbital Theater to orient geographically, then drop into any analytic tool that matches the question you're asking.

    Interactive tools · when to use each

    01
    Day-15 NSC Briefing capstone
    Use when you want context — the situation report the Joint Chiefs would hand to POTUS. Start here if you're new to the scenario.
    02
    Orbital Theater 3D spatial
    Use when you want to orient geographically. Zoom into the Strait of Hormuz, watch satellite orbits. Hit the HORMUZ preset for a close-up.
    03
    Principals Chamber deliberation
    Use when you want to see the debate. Enter a decision and watch six principals argue from their distinct utility functions, then converge.
    04
    Causal Cascade Bayesian
    Use when you want to trace consequences. Clamp any event to TRUE or FALSE and watch downstream probabilities update. Counterfactual mode shows per-node impact.
    05
    War Game campaign
    Use when you want to play the scenario yourself. Turn-based 10-week campaign. Each week make a military, diplomatic, and covert decision. Six actors respond.
    06
    Strategic Dashboard forecast
    Use when you want an analyst view. 4-slider scenario model for oil price, GDP impact, recession risk. 60:1 cost-asymmetry analysis.
    07
    Intel Workbench Monte Carlo
    Use when you want quantitative rigor. 10,000-trial Monte Carlo with p10/p50/p90 fan charts, Bayesian prediction updater, tornado sensitivity.
    08
    War Room agent-based
    Use when you want to model adversary reasoning. Six AI actors with utility functions play against your moves.
    09
    Weapons Explorer strike planner
    Use when you want to plan a strike package. 12 platforms × 18 targets with damage expectancy math. Knapsack optimizer finds the optimal loadout under budget.
    10
    Theater Map 2D ops
    Use when you want a flat map view. Pan and zoom with 13 toggleable layers including ISR coverage and time-distance rings.
    11
    Escalation Simulator game theory
    Use when you want to study the rungs. Ten-rung ladder with per-rung payoff matrices and Nash equilibrium solver.
