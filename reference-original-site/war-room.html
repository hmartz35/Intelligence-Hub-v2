Pre-game briefing · NSC principal

Your charter, as of Week 1

Fifteen days into Operation Rough Rider, Houthi attacks have declined 72%, but Iranian posture is hardening. VLS inventory is strained. Your coalition is fragmenting. You have 10 weeks to shape the endstate.

Each turn you may commit up to 2 actions across military, diplomatic, and covert domains. Five AI actors respond:

  • Iran — utility peaks at deterrence without direct war; risk-averse on homeland, permissive via proxies.
  • Houthis — utility trades attack tempo against regime survival; driven by Iranian resupply.
  • Hezbollah — preservation-first; Lebanon exposure is a heavy negative weight.
  • Israel — independent utility; may strike if Hezbollah mobilizes or if Iran enrichment accelerates.
  • Saudi Arabia — oil-disruption and Houthi-proximity drive posture; rarely forward.

Scoring combines final escalation rung, coalition cohesion, cumulative cost, civilian casualties, and Houthi degradation. Run is compared against three historical analogs. Shareable via seeded URL.
